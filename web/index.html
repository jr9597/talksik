Talksik

Hold, and sustain “ah”.

A small encoder compares one second of your voice with clinic recordings of healthy voices and three voice disorders. It runs in this browser. The recording stays here.

Hold the button, or hold the space bar. Let go when you have said a steady “ah”.

    Trained on sustained /a/ vowels from the Saarbrücken Voice Database (Pützer & Barry, CC BY 4.0). Speakers in the test score never appeared in training. This is a research demo, not a medical device, and not a diagnosis.
